Privacy Policy for HealthSync

Last updated: 2026-09-25

HealthSync is a personal, single-user utility built and used only by its developer. It is not distributed to or used by other end users.

What data is accessed

HealthSync connects to the developer's own Fitbit account via the Fitbit API and reads fitness data such as activity and step counts.

How the data is used

Retrieved data is written to the developer's own private personal data repository for personal record-keeping purposes only.

Data sharing

Data is never shared with, sold to, or made accessible to any third party.

Data retention

Data is retained in the developer's private repository until manually deleted by the developer.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to tomo1998a@gmail.com.
